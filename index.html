Paper forgets. People move on.

Every farm keeps records somewhere. The trouble is where they end up.

In VunaWise, every entry is saved the moment it’s made, belongs to the farm rather than the person who wrote it, and can be read from anywhere.

Made for mixed farms

Most Kenyan farms don’t do one thing. VunaWise keeps livestock and crops side by side, so you can compare what each part of the farm really earns.

  • PigsBreeding and fattening

    Services, farrowing, litter sizes, weaning weights, feed, treatments and sales. Deaths are recorded against the individual animal, so you see where losses happen and why.

  • CattleDairy and beef

    Herd register, milk records, breeding and calving, vaccinations and vet visits, with costs tied to the animals that incurred them.

  • PoultryChickens and ducks

    Flocks, egg collection, feed use, mortality and sales, so you know which flock is paying for itself.

  • CropsMaize, cabbage, sunflower and more

    Plots, planting dates, inputs, spraying, labour and harvests, with yield and cost per plot season after season.
