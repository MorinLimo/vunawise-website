How it works

From your first animal to your first harvest report.

1

Set up your farm

Create your VunaWise account. Add your farm name, your team, and your enterprises — pigs, cattle, crops, poultry.

2

Build your playbooks

Create lifecycle templates for your animals and crops. VunaWise calculates all task dates automatically from your planting or birth date.

3

Track daily operations

Record sales, expenses, treatments and activities from the web or your phone. Your dashboard updates in real time.

4

Make smarter decisions

See your net profit, identify your most productive enterprises, and plan your next season with confidence.
