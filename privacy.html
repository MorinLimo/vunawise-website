Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

VunaWise ("we," "us," or "our") provides a farm management platform, accessible via our mobile app and web dashboard (together, the "Service"), that helps farms track animals, treatments, expenses, sales, requisitions, and related records. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it's protected.

Information We Collect

When you use VunaWise, we collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and role (e.g. manager, farmer, admin), used to create and secure your login.
  • Farm records you enter: data you or your team input into the Service, including animal records, treatments and health events, expenses, sales, invoices, requisitions, and farm task/lifecycle plans ("playbooks"). This data belongs to your farm account and is not shared with other farms using the Service.
  • Authentication data: a secure login token is stored on your device to keep you signed in between sessions.

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, photos, or device identifiers beyond what's needed for standard app functionality.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate the Service — storing, displaying, and syncing your farm's records across the mobile app and web dashboard.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your farm's data separate and secure from other farms using VunaWise.
  • To improve the Service based on how it's used and to fix issues.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your farm records for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a secured database, accessed only through authenticated requests tied to your account and farm. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, not as plain text. Each farm's data is isolated at the database level, so one farm's records are never visible to another farm's users.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information or farm records with third parties, except:

  • With infrastructure providers that host our servers and database, solely to operate the Service (e.g. cloud hosting providers).
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of VunaWise, our users, or others.

Your Choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and farm data by contacting us using the details below. If you delete your account, your data will be removed from active systems, subject to any backups retained for a limited period for recovery purposes.

Children's Privacy

VunaWise is intended for use by farm operators, managers, and staff, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us
